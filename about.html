About Valexa

A cashback platform built around one idea: shopping you were already doing should give a little back.

Valexa connects everyday online shoppers with cashback from partner stores across fashion, electronics, travel, groceries, and more. When you shop through a Valexa offer, we track the purchase and pass a share of it back to you once the store confirms the order.

We built Valexa around a few plain commitments: every offer states its real rate and its real conditions, your dashboard always shows exactly where your cashback stands, and we never publish numbers we can't stand behind.

What we won't do

  • Advertise cashback rates we can't actually track through to a store.
  • Bury conditions that change what you'll actually receive.
  • Sell your shopping data to anyone outside of running Valexa itself.

Where we're headed

Valexa is actively expanding partner stores and building direct integrations so cashback tracking becomes fully automatic — no manual confirmation required. Our How It Works page always reflects how tracking currently functions.
