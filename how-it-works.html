How It Works

What happens between clicking an offer and cashback landing in your account.

1. Browse an offer and click through

Every offer on Valexa lists a store, a cashback rate or flat amount, and the conditions attached to it. When you click "Shop at [store]", you're taken to that store's own website to complete your purchase at the store's normal prices.

2. Confirm your purchase

After you've placed your order, return to Valexa and confirm the order amount on the offer page. This starts tracking for that purchase and logs it as pending in your Cashback History.

3. Pending review

Stores need time to confirm an order wasn't cancelled or returned. During this window your cashback stays marked as pending — this is standard across cashback platforms and protects against paying out on orders that don't go through.

4. Confirmed, then credited

Once the store's return or cancellation window closes, your cashback moves to confirmed. From there it's queued for credited status, at which point it reflects in your account balance.

A note on timing and eligibility

Cashback amounts and confirmation timelines are set by each partner store and vary by category — they're listed on every offer's own page. Orders placed with ad-blockers enabled, through incognito windows, or via coupon codes not listed on the offer can sometimes fail to track; if that happens, our Support team can help you look into it.
