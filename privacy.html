Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Policy explains what information Valexa collects and how it's used.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and optionally your mobile number.
  • Cashback activity: the offers you click through to and the purchases you confirm, so cashback can be tracked and paid.
  • Technical information: basic device and browser information used to keep the site secure and working correctly.

2. How we use this information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To track cashback on purchases made through Valexa and pay it out.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To keep the platform secure and prevent misuse.

3. What we don't do

We don't sell your personal information to third parties. We don't share your purchase history with anyone beyond what's needed to track and confirm your cashback with the relevant partner store.

4. Data retention

We keep account and cashback records for as long as your account is active, and as needed to meet legal and accounting obligations after that.

5. Your choices

You can update your account details any time from Profile & Settings. To close your account or request deletion of your data, contact Support.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical safeguards to protect your information, including hashed password storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we work to improve these protections over time.

7. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy periodically. Material changes will be reflected with a new "last updated" date above.

8. Contact

Questions about this Policy can be sent through our Contact & Support page.
