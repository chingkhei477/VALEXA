Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated:

1. Purchases happen at the partner store, not Valexa

Valexa doesn't sell products or process payments for the purchases you make. When you shop through a Valexa offer, your order, payment, refund, and cancellation are all handled directly by that store under its own policies.

2. If you cancel or return an order

Cancelling or returning an order placed through a Valexa offer will cancel the cashback tied to that order. This applies whether the cashback is still pending or has already reached confirmed status.

3. Cashback reversals

If cashback has already been credited to your account and the underlying order is later cancelled, returned, or found to be ineligible under the offer's terms, Valexa may deduct the equivalent amount from your account balance.

4. Cashback that doesn't track

If a qualifying purchase doesn't appear in your Cashback History within the timeframe shown on the offer page, contact Support with your order details. We'll look into it with the partner store, though resolution isn't guaranteed if the store's own records don't show the order as coming from Valexa.

5. No cash refunds from Valexa

Since Valexa doesn't charge you for purchases, there's nothing for us to refund directly — cashback is a benefit paid on qualifying orders, not a payment we've received from you.

6. Contact

Questions about a specific order or cashback entry can be sent through our Contact & Support page.
